How to Practice Gyan Mudra Meditation

Form the Gyan Mudra

Join the tip of your thumb and index finger, keeping the other three fingers straight but relaxed. This hand gesture symbolizes knowledge and wisdom.

Find a Comfortable Position

Sit in a comfortable position with your spine straight. You can sit on a chair or cross-legged on the floor. Rest your hands on your knees with palms facing up.

Close Your Eyes

Gently close your eyes to minimize visual distractions and turn your attention inward.

Focus on Your Breath

Bring your attention to your natural breathing pattern. Observe the inhalation and exhalation without trying to control it.

Maintain the Practice

Start with 5-10 minutes daily and gradually increase the duration as you become more comfortable with the practice.
